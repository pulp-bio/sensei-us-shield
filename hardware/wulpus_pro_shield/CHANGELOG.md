## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-04

### Added

* Initial WULPUS PRO shield PCB release for the BioGAP platform.
* 16-channel high-voltage multiplexer and transmit/receive protection.
* 30 V ultrasound pulser.
* Programmable receive chain with preamplifier, variable-gain amplification, and low-pass filtering.
* 12-bit, 8 MS/s ultrasound acquisition.
* Positive and negative high-voltage supplies supporting piezoelectric and CMUT transducers.
* BioGAP power, SPI, handshake, and MSP430 programming interfaces.
* Altium design sources and component libraries.
* Manufacturing files, including schematics, assembly drawings, BOM, Gerber files, NC drill files, and pick-and-place data.
